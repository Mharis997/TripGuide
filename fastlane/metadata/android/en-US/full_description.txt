Not sure where to go this weekend? Tell TripGuide about your trip and it suggests places that actually fit.

PLAN IN SECONDS
• Pick your departure and return time from home
• Set your total budget and how many people are going
• Choose how you travel – car, bus, train, flight or motorbike
• Pick the vibe: just escape, mountains, beaches, party, chill, nature, adventure, romantic, family, culture, food and more
TripGuide ranks every place by match score and shows travel time, estimated transport and stay costs, season fit, and any warnings – before you commit.

GO FULLY PREPARED
Every place comes with photos, how to get there, a day-by-day guide, local tips, safety and emergency numbers, best months to visit, amenities and a live 7-day weather forecast.

CONDITION UPDATES
Log what you find on the ground – temporarily closed, road blocked, weather alert, crowded or good news. The place's status badge changes instantly, closed or blocked places are flagged in suggestions, and your upcoming trips there get an alert.

FILTER YOUR WAY
Browse by category (mountains, valleys, lakes, beaches, historical, city…) and tick the amenities you need: parking, restrooms, hotels, camping, mobile signal, ATM, fuel, medical aid, wheelchair access, kid or pet friendly, 4x4 access and more. Sort by name, nearest or cheapest.

ADD YOUR OWN PLACES
Found a hidden gem? Add it with photos, location, guide, tips, amenities and best season.

YOUR TRIPS, ORGANISED
• Countdown and departure reminder
• Auto-generated packing checklist based on the destination and how you travel
• Budget tracker – log expenses and see what's left
• Trip notes, directions and one-tap sharing with your group

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your trips and places stay on your phone.
