Discover Kerala the way travellers actually share it – through reels.

SWIPE PLACES LIKE REELS
Explore opens as a vertical feed. Each place plays the newest Instagram reel travellers shared about it. Save it, tap Guide to navigate, or open the details.

GUIDES BUILT FROM REAL REELS
Every place collects what travellers' reels say: the most repeated tips, prices, timings and best months – so you arrive fully prepared.

READS MALAYALAM AND OTHER INDIAN LANGUAGES
TripGuide reads captions, text on screen and speech in Malayalam, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Hindi, Bengali and more – right on your phone, no account or AI needed. Tap Translate for English, or Ask my AI to send it to the AI app you already use.

SHARE A REEL, HELP EVERYONE
Saw a great Kerala reel? Tap Share in Instagram → TripGuide, or pick a saved video or photo. TripGuide reads it, links it to the right place (or adds a new one) and updates the guide for every traveller.

DON'T KNOW WHERE TO GO?
Tell TripGuide your departure and return time, budget, group size, transport and vibe – escape, mountains, beaches, party, chill, nature, culture, food and more. It ranks places that fit, with travel time, cost estimates, season fit and warnings.

ONE-TAP NAVIGATION
The Guide button starts Google Maps navigation straight to the place – ready for Android Auto.

SAVE AND ORGANISE
Favorites, Plan to go, Visited. Trips with countdown, reminders, packing checklist, budget tracker and notes.

FILTER YOUR WAY
Categories (mountains, backwaters, beaches, waterfalls, wildlife, heritage, temples, cities…) and amenity checkboxes (parking, restrooms, hotels, camping, mobile signal, ATM, fuel, medical aid, wheelchair access, kid and pet friendly, 4x4 access…).
