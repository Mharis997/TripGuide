TripGuide Community Rules

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Reels, places and photos you add to TripGuide are visible to every TripGuide user. By adding content you agree to these rules.

Only add

  • Public Instagram reels about real places someone can visit.
  • Places with accurate locations, and photos you took or have the right to share.

Never add

  • Hate, harassment, violence, sexual content, or anything illegal.
  • Private or restricted places, private homes, or personal information about anyone.
  • Spam, advertising, scams or misleading safety information.
  • Content you don't have the right to share.

Reporting

Use Report on any reel or community place. Content reported by three different users is hidden automatically and reviewed. Content that breaks these rules is removed.

Reels belong to their creators

TripGuide links to and embeds public reels through Instagram's official embed; the video stays on Instagram. Creators can ask for removal at harism1407@gmail.com.
