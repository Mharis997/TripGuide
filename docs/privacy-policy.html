TripGuide Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

TripGuide ("the app") is a Kerala trip-planning directory built from places and Instagram reels that travellers share. This policy explains what the app handles. In short: your trips, budgets and saved lists stay on your phone; reels and places you choose to add are public to all TripGuide users; there are no ads, no analytics and no accounts with your name or email.

Stays on your device

  • Trips, budgets, expenses, packing lists, notes, saved lists (Favorites, Plan to go, Visited) and condition updates you log.
  • Settings such as your display name, home city or home coordinates, currency and theme.

This data is not sent to us. It may be included in your device's own Android backup if enabled. Delete it in Settings → Delete all my data or by uninstalling.

Shared with all users (only when you add it)

  • Reels you add: the reel link, the place you link it to, and the reel's public caption, creator username and summary.
  • Places you add: name, region, coordinates, description, guide and tips text, categories and amenities, and up to 3 photos (compressed).
  • AI guides: a summary generated from the captions of all reels linked to a place.
  • Reports you make on reels or places.

Each item is stored with a random anonymous user ID (Firebase Anonymous Authentication) so you can edit or delete your own items. That ID is not linked to your name, email or phone number. You can delete reels and places you added from within the app; for anything else contact us below.

Permissions

  • Approximate location – only when you tap "Use my location" to set your home or a new place's coordinates. Your own location is never sent anywhere unless you save it as the coordinates of a place you share.
  • Notifications – trip reminders and alerts about places on your upcoming trips.
  • Photos and videos – through the Android photo picker; the app only sees what you pick. When you analyse a video, the app takes up to 8 still frames on your phone and sends only those frames (small JPEGs) to the TripGuide server for AI analysis; they are not stored. The video itself never leaves your phone.
  • Internet – reels, photos, weather and community content.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore, Authentication) stores shared content and the anonymous ID. Firebase privacy.
  • TripGuide server on Cloudflare Workers receives the reel link, caption and video frames you submit, reads the reel's public preview (caption and image) from Instagram, and sends that text and those images, or the captions of a place's reels, to Anthropic's Claude API to extract travel information. No personal data about you is included. Anthropic privacy · Cloudflare privacy.
  • Instagram (Meta) – reels play through Instagram's official embed inside the app; Instagram receives your IP address and may set cookies under its own policy.
  • Open-Meteo – destination coordinates for weather forecasts.
  • Wikimedia Commons – built-in place photos.
  • Google Maps – the Guide button hands the destination to the Google Maps app for navigation.

As with any web request, these services receive your device's IP address. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.

Retention and deletion

Shared content stays until you delete it, it is removed for breaking the community rules, or you ask us to remove it. Reels with three reports are hidden automatically.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
