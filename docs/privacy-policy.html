TripGuide Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

TripGuide ("the app") is a trip-planning app. This policy explains what information the app handles. In short: we do not collect, store or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data stored on your device

  • Trips, budgets, expenses, packing lists and notes you create.
  • Places you add and condition updates you post, including photos you choose to attach (copied into the app's private storage).
  • Settings such as your display name, home city or home coordinates, currency and theme.

This data is never sent to us. It may be included in your device's own Android backup if you have backup enabled in system settings. You can delete it at any time in Settings → Delete all my data, or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

  • Approximate location – only used when you tap "Use my current location" to set your home or a new place's coordinates. The result is saved on your device only and is never transmitted.
  • Notifications – used for trip reminders and alerts about places on your upcoming trips.
  • Photos – selected through the Android photo picker; the app only sees the photos you pick.
  • Internet – used to load place photos and weather forecasts.

Third-party services

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) – the coordinates of the destination you are viewing are sent to get a weather forecast. Your own location is not sent.
  • Google Maps Platform – Places API (places.googleapis.com) – to show attractions and places to stay, the coordinates of the destination you are viewing, or the text you type into Discover, are sent to Google. Your own location is not sent. Results and photos are shown live and not stored. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Booking.com / Google Hotels – only when you tap a price button, your browser opens their site with the destination, trip dates and number of guests.
  • Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org) – built-in place photos are loaded from Wikimedia servers.

As with any web request, these services receive your device's IP address. See their own privacy policies for details. The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs and requires no account.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If a future version adds online features (for example sharing updates with other travellers), this policy will be updated before that release.
